# KAN-449 — Google Places proof of concept, 100 calls

Run 2026-09-17 against the Portugal archive
(`overture-country-sources/PT/1ea48e22-9b0d-47a2-beb7-29f5203bc204.csv`),
residual of 4,205 generic-shopping rows. Budget: 100 Google calls, spent
exactly (1 smoke + 20 + 20 + 30 + 29). Nothing emitted; checkpoints live in
`outputs/google-PT-poc/` and are not committed.

## What was asked

| step | calls | rows | named by Google | usable type | resolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| control, Text Search (20 rows Foursquare/OSM already resolved) | 20 | 20 | 12 | 2 | 2 (1 agrees) |
| control, Nearby circle, same 20 rows | 20 | 20 | 4 | 2 | 2 (2 agree) |
| residual, 30 densest Nearby circles | 30 | 132 | 21 | 6 | **3** |
| residual, 29 rows alone, Text Search | 29 | 29 | 9 | 2 | **0** |

"Named" = a place within 400 m passing the name ladder at any rung;
"usable type" = Google's `primaryType` is something other than `store`.

## What it says

1. **Google calls half of these shops `store`.** On the control rows Text
   Search found the shop by exact name 12 times out of 20 and 8 of those 12
   carry `primaryType: store` — Google's own generic bucket, the same thing
   Overture's `shopping` is. Google knows the shop exists; it does not know
   what it sells either.
2. **Nearby circles do not work at this density.** 22 of 30 residual circles
   came back with the full 20 results, so the circle was truncated: Google
   has several times more typed shops in a 100 m circle than Overture has
   store-like rows. Sizing circles from the archive under-estimates Google's
   density; sizing them small enough to fit would put one residual row per
   circle again. Grouping also filters by `includedPrimaryTypes`, which by
   construction drops every `store`-typed place — the majority.
3. **The long tail is not on Google under these names.** 20 of the 29 rows
   alone had no Google place within 400 m sharing the name at all. These
   are Meta-sourced names; many are pages for businesses Google never
   listed or that closed.
4. Four types in the first draft of `googlePlaceTypes.json` were not real
   Places (New) types (`eyewear_store`, `stationery_store`,
   `beauty_supply_store`, `optician`); Nearby rejects the whole request on
   one unknown type. Removed; `building_materials_store` added (→ hardware).

## Projection

3 resolved of 161 residual rows looked up = **1.9%**. The full hybrid run
(3,229 calls, inside the free tier) would resolve on the order of **80
rows** of 4,205, each still needing review. Google is not the source that
types this residual; it types the shops Foursquare already typed.

## Follow-ups, 28 more calls

* `primaryTypeDisplayName` on 8 of the `store`-typed control rows: it is
  the localised `primaryType` ("Loja"), not a finer category. Google
  Maps' own category taxonomy is not exposed by the Places API.
* `businessStatus` on the 20 best-matched residual rows: 19
  `OPERATIONAL`, 1 `CLOSED_TEMPORARILY`, 0 `CLOSED_PERMANENTLY`. The
  shops Google finds are open; the closed ones would be among the rows
  it does not list, which the API cannot confirm.

Total: 128 calls.

## Decision

Stop. No full run. Google's API knows these shops exist, not what they
sell, and cannot confirm the absence it implies.

The one durable signal is absence itself: about 30% of the residual has
no Google listing under the Overture name at its coordinates, which for
Meta-sourced pages mostly means gone. The tool now emits that as
`unlisted` → an on-hold batch of reversible rejections, with
`CLOSED_PERMANENTLY` matches as `closed`, both validated by the CI gate.
It is there if the policy on the remaining residual ever wants it; it
is not scheduled.
